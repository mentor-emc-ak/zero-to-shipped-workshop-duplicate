About

I'm Guru, a UX designer. I believe an AI product earns its place the moment someone who didn't build it gets real value from it — saving time, gaining a skill, or solving something they couldn't before.

So I build the whole thing: the design, the reasoning, and the real working app. I keep a written record of each one so the next is better.

Capabilities

  • AI product design Turning a model into something a person can use.
  • UX research and testing Learning from real people, not from guesses.
  • Prototyping Fast, clickable builds to test the hard idea early.
  • Full build From the first sketch to an app that runs on its own.
